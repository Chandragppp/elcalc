# Privacy Policy — Panel Calc (`id.controlengineer.elcalc`)

**Effective Date / Tanggal Berlaku:** 2026-10-27 | **Developer:** ControlEngineerID

## English Version
1. **Local Processing & No Personal Data Collection**: **Panel Calc** works offline on your device. ControlEngineerID does not operate backend servers and does not collect, store, or sell your personal data, calculation inputs, or contacts. Trial and rewarded timestamps are stored locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore.
2. **Third-Party Google SDKs**:
   - **Google AdMob (`play-services-ads`) & UMP (`user-messaging-platform`)**: Used only to load optional Rewarded Video Ads when you tap *"Watch 1 ad: Pro for 24h"* and to manage consent forms where required. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID), coarse IP location, ad interactions, and diagnostics per the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
   - **Google Play Billing (`billing-ktx`)**: Processes the optional one-time purchase (`pro_lifetime`). Payment details are handled directly by Google Play.
3. **Permissions**: `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, and `AD_ID` are used solely by Google Play Billing and AdMob. Sharing calculation reports uses the system share sheet (`Intent.ACTION_SEND`) without storage permissions.

## Versi Bahasa Indonesia
1. **Pemrosesan Lokal & Tanpa Pengumpulan Data Pribadi**: **Panel Calc** memproses seluruh perhitungan listrik 100% di dalam perangkat Anda. ControlEngineerID tidak mengoperasikan server sendiri dan tidak mengumpulkan, menyimpan, atau menjual data pribadi maupun angka perhitungan Anda. Status uji coba 3 hari dan akses Pro disimpan secara lokal di perangkat melalui Jetpack DataStore.
2. **Layanan Pihak Ketiga (SDK Google)**:
   - **Google AdMob & UMP**: Hanya digunakan untuk menampilkan iklan video berimbalan saat Anda memilih *"Tonton 1 iklan: Pro 24 jam"* dan mengelola persetujuan privasi (UMP). SDK AdMob dapat mengumpulkan *Advertising ID*, perkiraan lokasi berbasis IP, interaksi iklan, dan diagnostik sesuai [Kebijakan Privasi Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
   - **Google Play Billing**: Memproses pembelian sekali bayar (`pro_lifetime`) secara langsung melalui Google Play.
3. **Panduan Formulir Data Safety Play Console (untuk Pemilik)**: Pilih **Yes** pada pengumpulan data karena SDK AdMob mengumpulkan *Approximate location*, *App interactions*, *Crash logs & Diagnostics*, dan *Device or other IDs (Advertising ID)*, serta Google Play Billing memproses *Purchase history*.
